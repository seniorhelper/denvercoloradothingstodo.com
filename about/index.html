Who we are

About this guide

Denver Colorado Things To Do is an independent visitor guide to the city of Denver and the best day trips from it, published by Eye To Ad Media in Denver.

Last updated October 10, 2026

What this site is

This website is a free, independent guide for people planning time in Denver, Colorado. It focuses on two things: what is worth doing inside the city, and which day trips from Denver are worth the drive. Our pages cover attractions, museums, parks, seasonal events, getting around and practical planning, with links to the official sources behind every important fact.

We are not a tourism bureau, a government agency or a ticket seller, and we are not affiliated with the City and County of Denver, Visit Denver, the National Park Service or any venue we write about.

Who publishes it

The site is owned and published by Eye To Ad Media, a small Denver-based digital publishing and marketing company run by Zach Wennstedt. Eye To Ad Media also publishes several other independent Colorado guides, including a statewide events guide and a Colorado camping guide. Each site is edited separately and focuses on one subject.

How the guide is written

Pages are researched from primary sources: venue and museum websites, city and state agencies, the National Park Service, the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, the Colorado Department of Transportation and similar organizations. Where facts change often, such as hours, prices and reservation rules, we say so and send you to the official page rather than repeating numbers that may go stale. We do not publish invented reviews, ratings, testimonials or claims of visits we did not make. Our full approach is on the how we research page.

How the site is funded

The guide is free to read. It may display advertising, including ads served by Google AdSense, to cover the cost of research and hosting. Advertisers have no influence over what we recommend, and nothing on this site is paid placement. If that ever changes for a specific item, it will be clearly labeled. Details on cookies and advertising are in our privacy policy.

Corrections and suggestions

Denver changes quickly. If you spot an error, a closed business, a changed rule or a broken link, please tell us through the contact page. We review every correction request and update pages when an official source confirms the change.

Important note

This guide is for general travel planning only. Conditions in the mountains, on roads and at venues can change without notice. Always confirm hours, closures, reservations and safety information with the official source before you go. See our terms and disclaimer.
