Privacy policy

This policy explains what information is collected when you use denvercoloradothingstodo.com, how advertising cookies work and what choices you have.

Last updated October 10, 2026

Who we are

Denver Colorado Things To Do (denvercoloradothingstodo.com) is published by Eye To Ad Media, Denver, Colorado. Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact page or to info@eyetoad.com.

Information we collect

Information you send us

If you use the contact form, we receive the name, email address and message you enter, along with the page you sent it from. The form is processed by FormSubmit, a third-party form service, which delivers the message to our inbox. We use this information only to respond to you and to improve the site. We do not sell it or add you to a marketing list.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider and any analytics or advertising services may automatically receive standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page and the pages you view. This information is used to operate the site, keep it secure, understand which guides are useful and display advertising.

Advertising and cookies

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Please read this section carefully:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.

If you opt out, you may still see ads, but they will be less tailored to your interests. You can learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services at Google's partner sites page.

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where required by law, including for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalized advertising cookies are used, through a consent message that uses a Google-certified consent management platform. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through the privacy or cookie settings link shown in that message. Without consent, Google may show non-personalized ads, which still use cookies for purposes such as frequency capping and fraud prevention.

Managing cookies in your browser

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. Blocking all cookies may affect how some websites work.

Links to other websites

Our guides link to official agencies, venues and other sites. Their privacy practices are their own, and we encourage you to read their policies.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or to object to certain processing. Colorado residents have rights under the Colorado Privacy Act where it applies. To make a request, contact us and we will respond within the time required by applicable law.

Data retention and security

We keep contact messages only as long as needed to respond and maintain records, and we use reasonable measures to protect them. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site or the law changes. The date at the top of the page shows the latest revision.
